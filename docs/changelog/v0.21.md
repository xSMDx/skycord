# v0.21 (draft, not released) — the Android app

A draft for the upcoming v0.21, the first Android release. It holds the Android app's lines, which were written for
0.20.99 and held back when the owner ruled (2026-11-02) that 0.20.99 does not ship the Android app. It uses the
voice of the landing page's `RELEASES` list (`landing/content.js`), with each line tagged **add**, **imp** (improved)
or **fix**. Nothing here is on the landing page yet; it goes there, with a title and date, when v0.21 is cut and
tagged `android-v0.21.0` (see `docs/RELEASING.md`). Review every line against the app as it is at that point.

The phone call screen, hold to talk on phones, the music card in a call and the phone layout fixes the app shows
already shipped in the web client in v0.20.99 (`docs/changelog/v0.20.99.md`), so they are not repeated here.

## Added

- **add** — **An Android app.** Install Skycord on an Android phone (Android 8 or newer) from the releases page. It
  opens your own server, or skycord.xyz, keeps a list of the servers you use, and updates itself. Links to other
  sites open in your browser, the back button closes whatever is on top before it leaves a chat, and files you attach
  come from the phone's own picker.
- **add** — **Notifications on Android, without Google.** The app keeps its own quiet connection to your last server,
  so messages, mentions and calls reach you even with Skycord closed. You get one notification per conversation, with
  every line, and you can reply from it. Tapping it opens that chat. If the phone was off for a while, it catches up
  on what you missed when it comes back. Your friends don't see you online just because the app is listening.
- **add** — **Calls ring like a phone call.** A DM or group call shows a full-screen incoming call with Answer and
  Decline, even on the lock screen. Answer opens the call, and a call you didn't pick up leaves a missed-call
  notification.
- **add** — **Back in a call.** In the app, pressing Back on the call screen keeps the call going and takes you back
  to chatting, with a bar at the top to return to the call.
- **add** — **The new logo on Android.** The headphones mark is the launcher icon and the notification icon.

## Improved

- **imp** — **Calls show the caller's display name** instead of their username on the ringing screen, the call
  screen and the missed-call notification.

## Fixed

- **fix** — **The Windows app's launch and update-check window showed the old logo.** Installs from before 0.20.99
  still open that window with the old mark. The window now shows the new one, and it no longer waits for the update
  check before the app opens. Updating once is enough.
- **fix** — **The member list on a phone** couldn't be closed with Back without leaving the channel.

## Notes for people who host their own server

- **Deploy the web client before handing out the Android app.** The app shows your server's own web client, and its
  phone screens come with v0.20.99 or newer.
- **Android notifications** use the app's own connection, not Google's push service. There is nothing to set up on
  the server.
