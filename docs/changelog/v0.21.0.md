# v0.21.0 — Skycord for Android (beta)

The changelog for v0.21.0, the first Android release, in the voice of the landing page's `RELEASES` list
(`landing/content.js`). Each line is tagged as it is there: **add**, **imp** (improved) or **fix**. It is three
tags (see `docs/RELEASING.md` and `android/README.md`): `v0.21.0` for the server and web client, `desktop-v0.21.0`
for the Windows app and `android-v0.21.0` for the Android app, in that order — the hosted server must serve the new
web client before the APK is published.

The Android lines were written for 0.20.99 and held back when the owner ruled (2026-11-02) that 0.20.99 does not
ship the Android app. The phone call screen, hold to talk on phones, the music card in a call and the phone layout
fixes the app shows already shipped in the web client in v0.20.99 (`docs/changelog/v0.20.99.md`), so they are not
repeated here. 0.21.0 rather than 0.20.101: the Android app's versionCode is `X*10000 + Y*100 + Z`, so its patch
and minor numbers must stay below 100.

Title: **Skycord for Android, in beta**. The Android app ships as a beta by the owner's choice (2026-11-08): the GitHub release is titled "(beta)" but is not marked a pre-release, because installed apps find updates through `releases/latest`, which skips pre-releases.

## Added

- **add** — **An Android app, in beta:** the first version, so tell us what goes wrong. Install Skycord on an Android phone (Android 8 or newer) from its releases page. It
  opens your own server, or skycord.xyz, keeps a list of the servers you use, and updates itself. Links to other
  sites open in your browser, the back button closes whatever is on top before it leaves a chat, and files you
  attach come from the phone's own picker. **If you host your own instance, update the server before handing out
  the app: it shows your server's own web client, and its phone screens come with v0.20.99 or newer.**
- **add** — **Notifications on Android, without Google.** The app keeps its own quiet connection to your last
  server, so messages, mentions and calls reach you even with Skycord closed. You get one notification per
  conversation, with every line, and you can reply from it. Tapping it opens that chat. If the phone was off for a
  while, it catches up on what you missed when it comes back. Your friends don't see you online just because the
  app is listening. There is nothing to set up on the server for it.
- **add** — **Calls ring like a phone call.** A DM or group call shows a full-screen incoming call with Answer and
  Decline, even on the lock screen. Answer opens the call, and a call you didn't pick up leaves a missed-call
  notification.
- **add** — **Back in a call.** In the app, pressing Back on the call screen keeps the call going and takes you back
  to chatting, with a bar at the top to return to the call.
- **add** — **The new logo on Android.** The headphones mark is the launcher icon and the notification icon.
- **add** — **Right-click a shared screen for its own menu:** its Stream Volume, even before you join, and Join
  stream or Leave stream, with the sharer's usual options below. Right-clicking the person, in the call or in the
  voice channel's list, offers Join stream and Leave stream too. Joining from any of them brings the stream to the
  front, and leaving puts the grid back.

## Improved

- **imp** — **Calls show the caller's display name** instead of their username on the ringing screen, the call
  screen and the missed-call notification.

## Fixed

- **fix** — **The Windows app's launch and update-check window showed the old logo.** Installs from before v0.20.99
  still opened it with the old mark. It now shows the new one, and it no longer waits for the update check before
  the app opens. Updating once is enough.
- **fix** — **On a phone, the member list** couldn't be closed with Back without leaving the channel.
