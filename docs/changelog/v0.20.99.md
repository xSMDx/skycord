# v0.20.99 — Calls in your pocket, music on hold

The changelog for v0.20.99, in the voice of the landing page's `RELEASES` list (`landing/content.js`). Each line
is tagged as it is there: **add**, **imp** (improved) or **fix**. The release is tagged `v0.20.99` (the server and
web client) and `desktop-v0.20.99` (Windows); see `docs/RELEASING.md`. **There is no Android release in 0.20.99**
(owner, 2026-11-02): the Android app's lines moved to the v0.21 draft, `docs/changelog/v0.21.md`.

Title: **Calls in your pocket, music on hold** (the landing page's title for this release; "Skycord on your
phone" is already v0.8's).

## Added

- **add** — **A call screen made for phones**, in the browser you already have. It is full screen, with big round buttons on a sheet at the bottom:
  mute, camera and hang up. Swipe the sheet up to see who is in the call, the music playing in it, Invite people and
  Voice settings.
  - When someone turns their camera on, their video fills the screen, with everyone else in small tiles, and the
    buttons step aside after a few seconds until you tap.
  - The person talking gets the big spot, and it doesn't jump around when two people talk over each other.
  - Tap the arrow to keep chatting: a bar at the top takes you back to the call.
  - The screen stays on while you're in a call.
- **add** — **Hold to talk on phones.** In a channel that uses push to talk, a big Hold to talk bar replaces the
  keyboard key you don't have. It asks for the microphone when you join, so the first press isn't interrupted, and
  it tells you if your voice isn't getting through.
- **add** — **Music in the phone call screen.** The Music card shows what's playing, with the cover and a title that
  slides when it's too long. Tap it for the call's music channels: tune in, add songs, skip, start or close a
  channel. On a phone, closing a channel takes two taps, because it stops the music for everyone.
- **add** — **Pick several songs at once.** Select songs in your library, then add them all to your queue or delete
  them, with one confirmation. In a playlist the same selection removes them from that playlist instead. A long
  run that reaches the server's limit waits and carries on by itself, and Cancel stops it.
- **add** — **Upload several songs at once.** Choose or drag in as many files as you like. They upload one after
  another, each with its own progress. If you hit the upload limit, Skycord waits and carries on by itself, and a
  file that fails doesn't stop the rest. If your connection drops, the rest wait for you, with a Retry button.
- **add** — **Reorder your queue.** Drag a song up or down by the handle on its left, with a mouse or a finger, or
  use the arrow keys on the handle.
- **add** — **Pause the music in a call.** A call's music channel can be paused and resumed for everyone listening,
  and it picks up where it stopped. **If you host your own instance, update the music service before the server to
  get Pause; with an older music service the button simply doesn't appear.**
- **add** — **Join a screen share to watch it.** Someone sharing their screen no longer starts downloading to
  everyone in the call. You see who is sharing and a Join stream button, and the stream only loads once you join.
  Leave it whenever you like without leaving the call. On a phone, tap the person in the call sheet to join or leave
  their stream, and the stream you join takes the screen.
- **add** — **Stream volume.** Right-click someone to turn their shared screen's sound down without touching their
  voice. On a phone, tap them in the call sheet, or long-press the stream you're watching.
- **add** — **A new logo.** The Skycord creature now wears headphones, because Skycord is first a place to talk. It's
  on the app icons, the browser tab, the loading screens, the Windows tray and taskbar, and the default avatars,
  which still blink. The new icon arrives with the update: Windows refreshes its icon cache as the update installs,
  and an installed web app picks it up the next time the browser checks.

## Improved

- **imp** — **The whole app works on a phone.**
  - Sign-up scrolls and every field fits.
  - Nothing slides sideways.
  - Settings, Server Settings, Notifications and the music player fit the screen.
  - Every control is big enough for a finger.
  - The member list is reachable in servers.
  - Interface zoom no longer shrinks the layout.
  - The grey tap flash is gone.
- **imp** — **The music player on a phone has its controls back**: the queue, seeking within a song, and a playlist
  strip you can actually see.
- **imp** — **A Push to Talk setting saved on a computer** no longer silences your microphone on a phone, which has no
  key to press.
- **imp** — **The push-to-talk key on a computer** now says why a press did nothing, when the microphone is
  blocked, missing or in use by another app, instead of failing silently. And a key you let go of before the
  microphone had come on no longer leaves it on.
- **imp** — **The call log names the caller by their display name** instead of their username.
- **imp** — **Your own profile** offers Edit profile instead of Message and Add friend.
- **imp** — **Uploading too many songs at once now says "Too many uploads — wait a moment"** instead of a message
  about profile updates. Profile, server and sticker uploads keep their own limit and wording.

## Fixed

- **fix** — **Joining a voice call no longer plays whatever song is on in it.** You hear a call's music channel only
  after you tap Listen. The person who starts or shares a song still hears it.
- **fix** — **The music queue lost its bottom half.** On a computer, scrolling down a full queue carried on into the
  library behind it and pushed the rest of the queue off the screen.
- **fix** — **Turning someone up past 100 %** could stop their volume applying at all. Now it makes them louder, up
  to 200 %. In a browser that can't send the louder sound to an output device you picked (anything but Default), the
  slider stops at 100 % and says "Max 100% with this output device".
- **fix** — **Your status could jump back to an old one** when another tab or device reported you idle.
- **fix** — **Leaving a group** didn't stop that group's live updates reaching you.
- **fix** — **Server Settings** flashed "isn't available yet" while it loaded.
- **fix** — **A lifted server mute** didn't put the person back on the air.

## Notes for people who host their own server

- **Update the music service before the server** if you want Pause in calls. Nothing breaks if you don't; the button
  just stays hidden.
