Press play for
the whole call.

Open a music channel in any voice call and everyone in it can tune in. The server plays it, so nobody has to share their screen or play songs into their mic.

  • Your own library: upload a pile of songs at once, or paste a link
  • Pick several songs and queue or delete them together
  • A shared queue anyone can add to, reorder, pause, skip or seek
  • Or type /play, /pause, /skip and /queue in chat
