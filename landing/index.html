What's inside

Video calls & screen share

Turn on your camera or share a screen mid-call. Sharing never loops the call's own audio back at your friends.

  • Big screen mode with spotlighted streams
  • Fullscreen and hide-chat options
  • Screen share with LIVE badge
  • Camera preview before going live

Voice calls & controls

Instant speaking rings, noise suppression, and full control over your audio setup.

  • RNNoise noise filtering
  • Input sensitivity as noise gate
  • Per-person volume controls
  • Live device switching without rejoining

Connection monitoring

Real-time diagnostics so you always know what's happening with your call.

  • Live ping graph and packet loss stats
  • Debug panel for codec and bitrate info
  • Signal meter with color indicators
  • Save diagnostics for bug reports

Customization & themes

Make Skycord yours with themes you can share and apply instantly.

  • Theme sharing in chat
  • Custom banners with GIF support
  • Custom status with timers
  • Profile page with all your settings

Groups & conversations

Rich chat features with full control over your conversations.

  • Group DMs with invite links
  • Pin and mute conversations
  • Reply threads and pins
  • Right-click menus everywhere

Fun & expression

Express yourself with stickers, GIFs, and reactions.

  • Custom stickers and emoji
  • GIF search
  • Emoji reactions
  • Rich text formatting

Channels & servers

Make a server for your crew, and give every topic its own room.

  • Text and voice channels, grouped in categories
  • Invite links — including straight into a voice channel
  • Member list with live presence
  • Timed statuses that expire on their own

Security & privacy

Your conversations are yours, with security hardening throughout.

  • Security hardening
  • Privacy controls
  • Encrypted calls coming soon
  • Self-host it yourself

Quick actions

Everything you need, right where you expect it.

  • Quick switcher
  • Jump to present button
  • Right-click context menus
  • Back to call button

What's being built, roughly in order. No dates — it ships when it's good.
