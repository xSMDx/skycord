What's inside

Video calls & screen share

Turn on your camera or share a screen mid-call. Sharing never loops the call's own audio back at your friends.

Instant speaking rings

Your ring lights the moment you talk — no waiting on a server round-trip to see who's speaking.

Switch devices live

Change mic, speaker, or camera from the call bar without rejoining. Test your setup right in the menu.

Themes you can send

Restyle Skycord, then drop the theme in chat. Friends preview it live and apply it with one click.

Group DMs & invites

Spin up a group, drop an invite link in any chat, and manage members without leaving the conversation.

Stickers, GIFs & reactions

Custom stickers, GIF search, emoji reactions, reply threads — the fun parts of chat, all in.
