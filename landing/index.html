Welcome to #download

Skycord for Windows: your servers in one app, and the screen-share tools a browser can't give you.

Skycord

The Windows app is out as a release candidate. Here it is:

Skycord for Windows
The latest release, on GitHub

What it adds over the browser:

  • Its own screen-share picker: a window or a whole screen, and the stream quality you want
  • A list of servers, yours and your friends', and switching between them in a click
  • It stays signed in, and updates itself when there's a new version

Windows may say it protected your PC: the installer isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Not on Windows? Skycord runs in your browser, right here, with nothing to install. Mac and Linux apps aren't made yet.

Video calls & screen share

Welcome to #host-your-own

Run Skycord on a machine you already own. Your crew signs in; only you ever see a terminal.

Skycord

One command on a fresh Linux server, and you have your own Skycord on HTTPS:

curl -fsSL https://skycord.xyz/install.sh | sudo bash

It asks for the address people will use and an email for certificate notices. Everything else it works out or generates.

Rather read it first? That's the point of it being one short script:

curl -fsSL https://skycord.xyz/install.sh -o install.sh
less install.sh
sudo bash install.sh

Updates are one command too, sudo skycord update, and an update that doesn't come up healthy puts the last version back by itself.

An old laptop is enough. The database runs on processors from before 2011, so the machine in your cupboard can host your crew, up to a hundred people a server.

Domains, certificates, backups and the rest are in the self-hosting guide.
